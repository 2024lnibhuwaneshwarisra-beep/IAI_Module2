Apply it · Case study

Design a university helpdesk assistant

Scenario: A university wants an AI helpdesk for first-year engineering students. It must answer admission and timetable questions politely, reply in Kannada or Hindi on request, avoid assumptions about the student, and answer only from the official handbook text. Write the prompt it needs, then explain which roles, advantages and ideas from this unit you used.

Show a model answer

Prompt: You are a friendly helpdesk assistant for first-year engineering students. Answer politely in simple English. If the student asks for Kannada or Hindi, reply in that language and keep course codes and units unchanged. Use only the text inside <handbook> tags. If the answer is not there, say “I could not find that in the handbook; please contact the office.” Do not assume the student's gender, region or background. Example: Student: “When does the Physics lab start?” Helpdesk: “According to the timetable, the Physics lab starts in Week 2.” Think first, then give your reply inside <answer> tags. <handbook>{{TEXT}}</handbook> Student question: {{QUESTION}}

Justification: Clarity: a role, tone and format are stated. Adapting context: formal but friendly helpdesk voice. Linguistic barriers: Kannada or Hindi on request, with codes and units kept. Ethics: no assumptions about the student. Question answering: answers come only from the handbook, with an “I could not find that” fallback (a conditional prompt). Data kept apart from instructions with tags (Tutorial Ch. 4), and an “out” to reduce invented answers (Tutorial Ch. 8). Future link: an adaptive version could change its tone based on how the student replies.
